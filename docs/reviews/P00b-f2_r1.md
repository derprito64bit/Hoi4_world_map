## Focus answers

1. **Will each build load for the reason under test?** Offline, yes: 0 ERROR, no X-crossings (wrap included), coastal flags unchanged, no province below 8 px, and no protected province (railway, supply node, adjacencies, adjacency_rules, naval-base sea province, 7855) lost a pixel. Railway steps stay adjacent. Moved lines sit on their own province's unchanged pixels with valid x/z and y. `naval_base_spawn` column 7 (index 6) sea provinces are protected. None of these lines, and no `floating_harbor` lines, moved. Interpretability of a failure is the P1 above.
2. **The `strip_problems` refactor** is equivalent. The body is moved verbatim. `widths[int(types[h])]` equals `width` for EXP-02, because both keys get the same value. The early return still returns only the host message plus the caller's earlier problems. The `coastal_flags` import moved to the top of the file. `forbidden` is now computed eagerly, which costs time only. `make`/`build` are untouched, so EXP-02 builds cannot change. `--check` passes on EXP-02-300/600/1200.
3. **`--check` is strict.** It checks the file set, including position files that are present but identical to vanilla, and every block and strip rule on the finished map. Per line, it catches lines left on a taken pixel, changed untouched lines, changes to other fields and wrong target provinces. The exceptions are the y gap (P2) and two weak negatives (P2). The other negative tests use specific needles and restore the file, and each re-checks to `[]` afterwards.
4. **Determinism.** No RNG is used. Donors are `sorted`. BFS order and the X-fix scan order are fixed. `interior_points` breaks ties by index. Sets are used only for membership. `test_rebuild_is_byte_identical` covers strip-full and block-400. No game files are in the diff.
5. **README.** It is clear and names the moved-line counts, the 8 or 89 new non-contiguous states, and the enclave pathing effect. It is missing the neighbour count and the strategic-region effect (P1), and the stale cannot-prove text (P2).

## Do not fix (looks odd, is correct)

- **Weather lines that land in another region's province.** Two lines are affected in block-400 (region 115 → province 3599 in region 176) and block-800 (region 111 → 2797 in 175). In vanilla these positions already lie on that province: 36 of 588 vanilla weather lines sit outside their own region. The relocation keeps the vanilla pixel's owner, which is the right conservative choice.
- **Unitstacks lines that now land in a different province than in vanilla.** This affects 3 lines in block-400 and 403 in block-800. Their vanilla position lay on another province's pixel. They are now moved into the province named in column 0, which is an improvement, and `--check` enforces it.
- **Strip-full cuts 171 crossed provinces into two parts, with fragments as small as 1 px.** EXP-02-600 had the same (1-px minimum fragment) and loaded. Multi-part provinces are legal (488 in vanilla).
- **Up to 37 unitstacks lines, or 13 buildings lines, share one pixel.** Every line of a small remnant goes to its single interior point. The positions are valid, and the game does not require them to be distinct.
- **`floating_harbor` column-7 provinces that are donors** (strip 4, block-400 2, block-800 3). They lose only pixels that are not next to the sea: the ring rule and the strip's neighbour rule see to that, and coastal flags are verified identical. The harbour positions did not move.
- **`registry.resolve` now compares with `e.exp_id.upper()`.** This is required so that `EXP-02b` resolves. `EXP-02` still does not pick EXP-02b builds (tested).
- **`STRIP_WIDTH` sea = 5625, not 5632.** The strip stops short of the seam, so the game and the validator measure the same box. Across the wrap, the minimal box is also 5625.
