**Conservativeness (item 3).** `unsafe_old` is character-for-character the old `unsafe`. `unsafe = unsafe_old or crash_g2` and `unknown` is now a superset (`seam and (fallback_strict or fallback_g2)`), so `guard_failures` can only grow. Nothing previously flagged becomes safe. Vanilla's unknown regions are still exactly [88, 95, 96, 97, 178, 180].

**The "grid mean = old mean or +1" property is right, and it holds in general, not only in the sampled boxes.** Per box, the old centre is ⌊(a+b+1)/2⌋ and the grid centre is ⌊a/2⌋+⌊b/2⌋+1 (a = xmin, b = xmax). They differ by +1 exactly when a and b are both even, and are equal otherwise. So the sum grows by d with 0 ≤ d ≤ n, and for non-negative sums ⌊(S+d)/n⌋ − ⌊S/n⌋ ∈ {0, 1}. The second claim (`clear` ⇒ `not fallback_g2`) is also a theorem: margin ≥ 1 gives bx0 ≤ xmin < M_g2 and bx0 + bw ≥ xmax + 1 ≥ M + 2 > M_g2. So a generator that keeps the ≥ 1 px margin rule (P00b rule 1) is safe under both models.
