# P00d — Hybrid Equal Earth projection (DEC-035), started 2026-09-30

## Why
Owner decision DEC-035 (Q-012): Equal Earth look, but seamless like vanilla. Pure Equal Earth's edge meridians are curves, so the left and right map edges only touch near the equator (EXP-09: off-globe wedge, lake filler traps fleets; EXP-01: adjacency links across the seam crash or are measured unwrapped). The hybrid keeps Equal Earth in the interior and blends to straight edges near the Pacific seam, so HOI4's native wrap (pixel contact at x=0/x=W−1) works over the full height.
